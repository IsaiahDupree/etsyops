Create your EtsyOps account

You're signed in — your EtsyOps account is ready. Everything runs in the cloud: there's nothing to install and nothing to run on your computer.

EtsyOps only ever works with the one Etsy shop you connect, and only with the permissions you grant. It never touches anything else on your computer or other accounts.

2

Add EtsyOps to your AI assistant

Add EtsyOps as a custom MCP connector in ChatGPT, Claude, or Codex (one time). Paste this server URL:

https://etsyops.vercel.app/mcp
  1. In ChatGPT, open Settings → Connectors and enable Developer mode (in Claude or Codex, open the MCP / custom-connector settings).
  2. Choose Add a custom connector and paste the MCP Server URL above.
  3. Set Authentication to OAuth and save.
3

Sign in & authorize

The first time your assistant uses EtsyOps, it sends you here to Sign in and click Allow. You then connect your Etsy shop once (a standard Etsy sign-in) so EtsyOps can audit and improve your listings.

  1. Your assistant opens the EtsyOps authorization page.
  2. Sign in with this same account and click Allow.
  3. Connect your Etsy shop when prompted — EtsyOps's tools are now available in chat.
4

Optimize your shop in chat

Start a new chat, enable the EtsyOps connector, and ask:

Audit my Etsy shop and show my worst-performing listings
Rewrite the SEO title and tags for my mug listing
Summarize my shop's revenue and fees over the last 30 days
What are buyers complaining about in my reviews? (paid)

Every write is a draft you review — EtsyOps creates drafts and proposes edits, and never publishes or changes a listing without your say-so. Creating and updating listings and mining reviews require a paid plan.

Connect your Etsy shop

Checking your Etsy connection…

EtsyOps connects to the one shop you authorize, over Etsy's official API. It reads your listings, orders, and reviews to audit and improve them; creating or updating a listing always happens as a draft or edit you review — nothing is published automatically.
