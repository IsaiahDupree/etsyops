Getting started

  • Create your account at etsyops.vercel.app — everything runs in the cloud, so there's nothing to install.
  • Add EtsyOps to your assistant. In ChatGPT, Claude, or Codex, add a custom MCP connector with the server URL https://etsyops.vercel.app/mcp and set authentication to OAuth.
  • Authorize by signing in with your EtsyOps account and clicking Allow. Then connect your Etsy shop with a one-time Etsy authorization and ask your assistant to "audit my shop" or "rewrite the SEO for my mug listing."

Common issues

  • Connector shows unauthorized. Remove and re-add the EtsyOps connector in your assistant to refresh the OAuth grant, then sign in and click Allow again.
  • "You've hit your plan limit." The Free plan includes 5 audit/content operations per month. Upgrade to Seller, Growth, or Agency from the plans page for unlimited audits and content.
  • A tool says it needs a paid plan. Creating draft listings and updating listings require the Seller plan or above; review mining and listing experiments require Growth or above. Searching, auditing, generating content, analyzing performance, and drafting replies work on the Free plan, within its monthly cap.
  • Your shop data looks incomplete or out of date. EtsyOps reads your shop through the official Etsy Open API v3; some figures (like views, favorites, and estimated Etsy fees) are Etsy's own or estimated and can refresh on a delay.
  • The numbers look like a sample, not my shop. If no Etsy shop is connected yet, EtsyOps serves a built-in sample shop so you can try it out. Connect your own shop with a one-time Etsy authorization to see your real listings, orders, and reviews.

Cancel / revoke access

  • Remove the EtsyOps connector in your assistant's settings to stop it from calling EtsyOps.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription anytime from the Stripe customer portal via the plans page.
  • Email us to delete your account and all associated data.
