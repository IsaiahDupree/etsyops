What EtsyOps does

EtsyOps is an AI Etsy shop operator. It lets an AI assistant audit your Etsy listings, generate SEO-ready titles, tags, and descriptions, analyze your shop's performance and reviews, draft customer replies, and — on paid plans — create and update listings on your behalf, all over the official Etsy Open API v3. EtsyOps runs entirely in the cloud; there is nothing to install, and it only ever accesses the one Etsy shop you connect, with your permission — never your other accounts, files, or devices.
